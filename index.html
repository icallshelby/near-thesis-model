The bull case, in evidence

  • Usage is real and cumulative: 5,477,965,357 lifetime transactions, 371 nodes online, blocks every ~0.566s across 1 shards (152,650 blocks/day).
  • Holders commit: 580,980,506 NEAR — 44.31% of all supply — is staked across 339 validators instead of sitting on exchanges.
  • Dilution is mostly behind it: 95.32% of total supply already circulates.
  • Liquidity is large-cap grade: 24h volume $533,051,366 = 11.53% of market cap — among the deepest turnover in the peer table below.
  • De-rated, not dead: -82.73% below the $20.44 ATH, yet the 365-day return is 15.5% and the network metrics above kept compounding.

The bear case, honestly

  • Stake is concentrated at the top: Nakamoto coefficient is 9 — the top 9 pools together hold over a third of all stake (top 10: 36.25%).
  • Volatility is extreme: 102.8% annualized over the last year, with a -68.4% maximum drawdown inside the window. Position sizing is not optional.
  • Fee revenue is not in this model: only metrics public APIs report directly are used; protocol fee income and burn are future work (see README).

Method & sources

  • NearBlocks API — supply, price, market cap, volume, lifetime transactions, nodes online.
  • NEAR mainnet RPC — chain status and the live validator set; stake shares and the Nakamoto coefficient are summed directly.
  • CoinGecko — 365 days of daily closes and the L1 peer set.
  • Raw responses committed in data/snapshot_raw.json; derived values in data/metrics.json.

Re-run it yourself: python3 model.py && python3 build_dashboard.py — Python 3 standard library only, no API keys.
